About BAHANTAS

This system provides flood risk assessment for four Metro Manila cities (Manila, Marikina, Pasig, and Quezon City) using historical weather data and machine learning. It serves as an analytical tool, not an official flood warning system .

Regression Model
Predicts the expected flood depth in meters derived from current weather conditions.
Classification Model
Predicts the risk level (Low, Moderate, High) based on MGB flood susceptibility thresholds.

Why Decision Trees?

Flooding is unpredictable. A slight drizzle might cause no issues, but a sudden burst of heavy rain or an overflowing river can flood a street in minutes.

Decision trees excel at handling these sudden, indirect patterns. They evaluate multiple weather conditions at once, making it easy to understand the conditions and logic behind every prediction.

Data Sources

  • Kaggle - Metro Manila Flood Prediction (2016–2020)
  • Open-Meteo - live weather and forecasts
  • Mines and Geosciences Bureau - flood susceptibility thresholds
  • News archives and LGU resources - flood event reports, evacuation centers and hotlines

Things to Keep in Mind

This system provides estimated flood risk based on historical weather patterns and machine learning models.
Keep these key points in mind when interpreting the results:

Assessments currently cover four localized target areas: Manila, Quezon City, Pasig, and Marikina.
River water levels rely on historical estimates due to the unavailability of live public monitoring data.
Confidence scores represent model classification certainty, not a guaranteed probability of flooding.
This is an analytical tool, official alerts must always be sourced from PAGASA and your local LGU.
