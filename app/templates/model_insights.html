Summary

Both models achieve strong performance on the historical dataset. Given the class distribution shown above (98.5% Low, 1.5% Moderate+High combined), high accuracy and low error metrics are expected and should be interpreted in that context. The macro-averaged recall is reported as the priority classification metric because missing a flood event is more costly than a false alarm.
