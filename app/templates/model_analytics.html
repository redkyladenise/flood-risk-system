Model Analytics

Technical evaluation and architectural breakdowns of the predictive CART models used for flood risk assessment.

Model Evaluation Summary

Both models perform strongly on the historical dataset. Rather than following a standard normal distribution, weather and flood data is naturally zero-inflated and right-skewed: dry days dominate the record (98.5% Low risk), while dangerous floods (1.5% Moderate and High combined) represent rare, extreme events.

The high evaluation scores, including a 0.93 Quadratic Cohen's Kappa score, occur because flooding follows clear physical rules that align naturally with the CART algorithm. Heavy rain volume and rising river water levels create distinct cutoffs that the decision tree separates easily, without target data leakage and with minimum sample rules (min_samples_leaf=20) to prevent overfitting. Macro-averaged recall (97.30%) is prioritized as our primary evaluation metric to prove the model actively and reliably detects life-threatening flood events rather than defaulting to majority-class predictions.
